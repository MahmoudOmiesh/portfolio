Sputnik Academy teaches kids about space, and it wanted to start selling its courses online. During my internship, four of us interns built its website, where families buy space courses and game kits for kids aged 6 to 14, and watch the lessons. I built most of what students see and all of the checkout.

## What I built

- **Checkout and orders.** Card and mobile wallet payments through Paymob, coupons, and shipping orders for game kits. The server works out the price from the cart itself and checks that each payment report really came from Paymob before it gives access to a course. Admins see every order and update its status. Paymob's docs left a lot out, and some of what they did say was wrong, so this took longer than it looks.
- **The course viewer.** Video lessons, section quizzes graded on the server, reviews, and progress. When a video ends, the lesson is marked done and the next one opens.
- **Browsing and buying.** The course list with filters and pages, course previews, game kit pages, the cart, and pages for your courses and orders.
- **The admin side.** Editors for lessons and quizzes, publishing courses, instructors and tags, coupons and game kits, and a page per student with their progress, quiz answers and reviews.
- **Uploads.** The server side of uploading lesson videos to Mux and files to S3.

Others on the team built sign-in, most of the landing page, and the signed links that play the lesson videos.

**Stack.** React, TanStack Router and Query, Hono on Bun, Prisma and Postgres, Kinde, Paymob, Mux, S3, and Turborepo.

## What I learned

This was my first internship, so I learned how to work on a team, meet deadlines, take part in meetings and talk to the people we were building for. Building software turned out to be as much about working with people as about writing code.
